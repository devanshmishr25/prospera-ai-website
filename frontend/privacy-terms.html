1. Privacy Policy & Data Protection Active

Prospera AI Realtors ("Prospera AI", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding the personal, financial, and requirement information of our clients. This document details our data handling procedures across all web properties, AI conversational engines, and direct developer communication channels.

1.1 Information We Collect

We collect only the essential information required to deliver high-precision real estate discovery and builder connectivity:

  • Identity & Contact Records: Full name, phone number, email address, and preferred micro-market city shared during consultations.
  • Property Requirement Parameters: Budget ranges, BHK configuration, investment timeline, and financing preferences communicated via voice or text.
  • Technical Session Metadata: IP address, device telemetry, browser type, and interaction session timestamps for fraud prevention.

1.2 How We Utilize Your Information

Your information is utilized solely to provide verified developer connectivity, customize AI property recommendations, schedule private site inspections, and fulfill regulatory compliance obligations under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act (RERA).

1.3 Zero-Spam & Non-Disclosure Commitment

We maintain an uncompromising privacy standard: we never sell, lease, or monetize your contact records to unauthorized telemarketers or third-party lead brokers. Context is shared exclusively with official developer sales teams for projects you explicitly select.

1.4 Data Security & Encryption Standards

All customer interactions, conversational transcripts, and inquiries are protected using 256-bit TLS/SSL encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Regular security audits ensure your data integrity remains fortified against unauthorized access.

2. Terms of Service & Platform Access Binding

By accessing or utilizing Prospera AI Realtors, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions. If you do not accept these terms in full, you must discontinue use of the platform.

2.1 Scope of Service & Zero Brokerage

Prospera AI serves as an intelligent advisory and discovery platform connecting prospective buyers directly with accredited real estate developers. On primary developer transactions, Prospera AI charges zero brokerage fees to the end consumer.

2.2 User Responsibilities

  • You agree to provide true, current, and accurate contact details when submitting inquiries or scheduling developer site visits.
  • You agree not to deploy automated crawlers, scrapers, or reverse-engineering scripts against our conversational AI models or proprietary property valuation databases.
  • You will not engage in fraudulent site visit requests or impersonate other individuals or commercial entities.

2.3 Intellectual Property Rights

2.4 Limitation of Liability

While Prospera AI works diligently to ensure property pricing, possession schedules, and project specifications reflect official developer records, Prospera AI shall not be held liable for discrepancies resulting from independent developer updates or force majeure events.

3. RERA & Regulatory Disclaimers Mandatory

Prospera AI operates in strict accordance with the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and applicable state RERA regulatory authorities.

3.1 Verified Developer Listings

All projects displayed or recommended by our AI advisory agents include verified RERA registration numbers from their respective state authorities (including UP RERA, Haryana RERA, and Delhi RERA). Prospects are advised to verify official details on the relevant state RERA portal.

3.2 Independent Due Diligence

Prospera AI provides predictive market insights, historical appreciation metrics, and AI recommendations for informational and research guidance. Nothing on this website constitutes legal, taxation, or certified financial advice. Prospective buyers must conduct independent legal title verification prior to signing binding developer agreements.

4. Cookie Policy & AI Voice Sessions Technical

We utilize standard session cookies and local storage tokens to provide a seamless conversational experience and remember your location preferences across browsing sessions.

4.1 Essential Functional Cookies

These cookies are necessary for core platform features, including conversational thread persistence, voice input processing, and user theme preferences. They do not store personally identifiable data outside encrypted tokens.

4.2 Realtime Voice Interaction Terms

When using our interactive Voice AI Concierge, audio streams are processed in real-time solely to parse your property criteria. Audio recordings are not repurposed for third-party commercial marketing.
