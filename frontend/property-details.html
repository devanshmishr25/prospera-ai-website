Property Overview

Verified Project

M3M Golfestate Crown is a premier luxury residential development located on Golf Course Extension Road, Gurgaon. Designed for connoisseurs of fine living, it offers expansive residences enveloped by manicured designer greens, unmatched golf course views, and direct connectivity to Delhi-NCR's top corporate hubs.

Featuring double-height grand arrival lobbies, private elevator access for select residences, VRV temperature control, and expansive wrap-around sundecks, this masterwork combines architectural grandeur with low-density residential serenity. Residents enjoy access to a 75,000+ sq.ft. signature clubhouse offering world-class recreation, culinary dining, wellness spas, and championship sports courts.
